Arriving late and leaving early after breakfast didnt leave much time to look around. But from the harbour the ship was an impressive site. 



{{< image src="/img/2025/06/17/IMG_4548.JPEG" caption="Only about 20 minutes from the harbour we were in wilderness" >}}

It was soon time to pack up and move on. Although we were in the middle of the harbour it wasn't long before we left the city behind and arrived in the Swedish countryside. 


{{< image src="/img/2025/06/17/IMG_4553.JPEG" caption="The first coffee stop of the day" >}}

The trail was very non technical hard packed gravel but there was a real feeling of being in the middle of nowhere. Apart from the occasional Trophy participant we saw no-one. 

{{< image src="/img/2025/06/17/IMG_4559.JPEG" caption="The trail passed many lakes and rivers" >}}


{{< image src="/img/2025/06/17/IMG_4568.JPEG" caption="It really felt like a very quiet, remote wilderness" >}}

After a long and very fast day on the trail, we arrived at Ténéré town. It was time to put the tent up, take a shower and enjoy the evenings entertainment.

{{< image src="/img/2025/06/17/IMG_4573.JPEG" caption="That evening's dinner was local game" >}}
